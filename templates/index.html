About the Dashboard

The CAHL Dashboard follows the Chiller Adult Hockey League — Columbus, Ohio's beer-league hockey home at the OhioHealth Chiller rinks. It exists for one reason: league stats live on a desktop-era site, and players check scores on their phones in the parking lot.

Everything here syncs from ChillerStats — the same scoresheets the league uses — refreshed continuously on game nights and archived for all-time player histories. Your saved team pins next game, recent result, and season record right to the top.

Not affiliated with the Chiller or the Columbus Blue Jackets. Just fans with a database.
